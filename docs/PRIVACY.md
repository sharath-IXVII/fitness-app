# FitAlarm privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

## Summary

FitAlarm keeps your fitness data on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what's needed to sign you in. We don't sell data, show ads or use third-party analytics.

## Your account

When you sign in with your phone number or Google, Firebase Authentication (Google) stores your phone number or email address, your name if Google provides it, and a user ID. This is used only to sign you in.

## Data stored on your phone

Your profile (age, height, weight, goal, diet), daily logs (workouts, water, steps, meals, protein), alarms, routines, your My Day plan, weigh-ins, weight goal, saved foods and meal photos are stored only on your device. We don't keep a copy on any server. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset data on this device" removes them.

## Camera during workouts

The workout camera counts reps using on-device pose detection (Google ML Kit). Video is processed in memory and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

## Food photos

Food scanning happens entirely on your phone using a built-in recognition model. Meal photos are never uploaded; they are kept only on your phone, next to the meal you logged.

## Steps, activity and location

Step counts and activity recognition (walking, cycling, in a vehicle) come from your phone's sensors and stay on your device. Location is used only while you record a GPS walk, to measure distance, and is not stored or sent anywhere.

## Notifications and alarms

Alarms and reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification content is sent to a server.

## Deleting your data

Use "Delete account" in the Me tab to permanently delete your sign-in and clear all data on the device.

## Children

FitAlarm is not directed at children under 13.
