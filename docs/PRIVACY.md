# Fit Tracker privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

## Summary

Fit Tracker keeps your fitness data on your phone. Only two things leave your device: what's needed to sign you in, and, if you use Friends, a small public profile and your challenges. We don't sell data, show ads or use third-party analytics.

## Your account

When you sign in with your phone number or Google, Firebase Authentication (Google) stores your phone number or email address, your name if Google provides it, and a user ID. This is used only to sign you in.

## Friends and challenges

If you use Friends, Cloud Firestore (Google) stores a public profile that your friends can see: your name, your best arc badge, level and XP, current and best streak, active days in the current arc and workouts this week. It also stores your friend list, people you blocked, challenges you send or join (names, exercise, target and whether each of you finished) and reports you make. Anyone you give a challenge code to can see that challenge. Nothing else (weight, meals, photos, location, health details) is shared.

## Data stored on your phone

Your profile (age, height, weight, goal, diet), daily logs (workouts, water, steps, meals, protein), alarms, routines, your My Day plan, weigh-ins, weight goal, saved foods and meal photos are stored only on your device. We don't keep a copy on any server, so signing in on a new phone starts fresh. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset data on this device" removes them.

## Camera during workouts

The workout camera counts reps using on-device pose detection (Google ML Kit). Video is processed in memory and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

## Food photos

Food scanning happens entirely on your phone using a built-in recognition model. Meal photos are never uploaded; they are kept only on your phone, next to the meal you logged.

## Steps, activity and location

Step counts and activity recognition (walking, cycling, in a vehicle) come from your phone's sensors and stay on your device. Location is optional: if you turn on "Use location for accuracy" or record a walk, GPS is used while the app is open to measure distance and improve step counts. Positions are processed on the phone and are never stored or sent anywhere.

## Notifications and alarms

Alarms and reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification content is sent to a server.

## Deleting your data

Use "Delete account" in your profile (tap your picture on Today) to permanently delete your sign-in, your Friends profile, friend list and challenges, and all Fit Tracker data on the phone. You can also email us to have your account deleted.

## Children

Fit Tracker is not directed at children under 13.
